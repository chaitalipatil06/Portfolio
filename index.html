How I brew a good product

Every design choice starts with who's on the other side of the screen: an irrigator checking the schedule and systems health early in the morning on our Water Informatics app, an HR clerk updating hundreds of records, a student stuck on recursion.

  1. 1

    Listen first

    Before redesigning our support process, I looked at where tickets actually got stuck. That's where the three-tier Zoho workflow came from.

    Pick the right tool

  3. 3

    Make it for everyone

    Accessibility is the base, not the garnish. My public-health website work started from ADA compliance instead of ending with it.

Things I've built

Product idea · Ignition Perspective

Flow Meter Health Score

A feature I proposed at Farm Data Systems: score each flow meter's health from flow rate and flow total during irrigation, so growers spot problems before they waste water.

Read the story
The problem
Raw flow numbers don't tell a grower whether a meter is healthy. Spotting a failing one meant digging through readings.
What I did
Proposed combining flow rate and flow total during irrigation into one easy health score, as part of the Ignition Vision and Perspective redesign.
Why it matters
One glanceable signal instead of a spreadsheet of numbers, built around how growers actually check their systems.

C# · Blazor · SQL Server · Telerik

HR tools for 36 school districts

Employee Update, Evaluations and Employment History workflows, plus SQL-backed reports, built while moving legacy ERP screens into Blazor.

Read the story
The problem
HR and payroll staff across 36 districts were working in older ERP workflows, and some ready-made components couldn't do what they needed.
What I did
Built tabbed Employee Update flows with modals and dynamic navigation, report-specific data models in Telerik Report Designer, and swapped constrained components for custom HTML, CSS and Blazor. Shipped through the CI/CD pipeline.
What I learned
The right component is the one that fits the user's task, not the one that came in the box.

Ops design · Zoho Desk · Confluence

Three-tier support workflow

Escalation tiers, clear ownership and dashboards, so support and management finally see the same picture of ticket volume and resolution.

Read the story
The problem
Tickets had no clear owner or escalation path, and managers couldn't easily see what was piling up.
What I did
Designed and introduced a three-tier workflow in Zoho Desk, built dashboards for tier-level activity, and standardized troubleshooting docs in Confluence.
Why it matters
Customers get routed to the right person faster, and the team can see where help is needed.
